```{admonition} Learning objectives
After this chapter you should be able to:
- describe a computer as a sensing–processing–acting system built on binary hardware;
- contrast programmed (rule-based) and learned (data-driven) behaviour and why it matters for interaction;
- list the sensors and actuators of modern devices and the latency/bandwidth they bring;
```

## Planned sections

1. From binary to behaviour: what a computer can and cannot 'perceive'
2. Sensors: cameras, microphones, IMUs, touch, biosignals, radar, LiDAR
3. Output: displays, speakers, haptic actuators, e-ink, projection
4. Latency, bandwidth, and why 100 ms is a magic number
5. Programmed vs. learned systems: predictability, error, and explainability
6. Edge vs. cloud: where the computation happens, privacy, and energy
7. A brief history: batch → terminal → GUI → mobile → ambient → AI-mediated (timeline figure)
